BEFORE THE DEMO
Open the site and check /healthz. Confirm AI with a real question in a separate
rehearsal conversation: "ai: configured" alone is not a provider connectivity test.
Use a fresh demo conversation and keep the manual Explorer available as fallback.
The model's wording/tool sequence may vary; demonstrate evidence, not a scripted answer.

0:00–0:25 | One workspace, several kinds of evidence
Open /overview: BFS industry vacancies, Eurostat occupational ads, SECO registrations
and SEM participant context each have their own period and unit.
Briefly open /opportunities: change IT Support to Data Analyst. Only the role panel
changes; junior and technology evidence have separate scopes.

0:25–0:50 | Manual investigation
Open /explore, Skills, ITBoard, Switzerland. Select Python and Compare with Docker.
Show Share, Count, the Data table and source drawer. These are active-ad snapshots
from one platform, not the entire Swiss market. The selector exposes all ten
datasets, but only ITBoard supports named skills.

0:50–1:40 | Ask, calculate, interpret
Choose Ask about this view:
  "How has Python changed in the last two available snapshots?"
Follow up:
  "Compare with Docker. Explain counts and shares."
Watch status updates, verified results and the linked chart. The final narrative
appears after validation; unvalidated answer tokens are not streamed.
For this frozen release, 29 October to 15 November 2026:
  Python: 115/582 to 188/770 ads; share about 19.76% to 24.42%.
  Docker: 99/582 to 123/770 ads; share about 17.01% to 15.97%.
Docker's count rises while its share falls. The changing denominator matters.

1:40–2:05 | An honest boundary
Ask: "And for junior developers?"
The analyst should explain that junior-by-skill data is unavailable. A separate
junior indicator may be offered, clearly labelled as a different scope.
No guessed values or unsupported filter should be presented as the requested answer.

2:05–2:45 | Participant context
Open /participants. Choose Refugees with permit B and Zürich canton.
Show the snapshot: 7,051 working-age people, 3,138 registered employed,
published rate 44.5%. Choose Ask about this brief, then ask:
  "What about Bern? What can we infer, and what data is missing?"
The group should remain refugee B while the canton changes. These are all-sector
population figures, not IT placements, programme outcomes or a trend.

2:45–3:00 | Verify and continue
Open a cited result and its source link. Show that it is the same result available
in the Explorer. Saved questions, results and decision context survive reload in
this browser; the context design supports the initial question plus 20 follow-ups.

OPTIONAL EXTENSION (outside the three-minute core)
Ask: "Should Powercoders expand its AI training programme?"
The analyst can inspect the ML / AI tag and approved research notes, but should
identify missing partner requirements, programme outcomes, costs and capacity.
Expect a conditional investigation or pilot suggestion, not an unsupported yes/no.

FALLBACK
If AI is unavailable or times out, show the same table, brief and sources manually.
State that the AI step is unavailable; do not present a prior answer as a fresh
live response. The dataset is a frozen release.
