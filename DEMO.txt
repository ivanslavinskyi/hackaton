Market Radar — three-minute demo

0:00–0:40 | See the sources, then explore
Start with Market overview: BFS industry vacancies, Eurostat occupational ads,
SECO registrations and SEM participant context. Each has its own period and unit.
Open Data library to show national and regional coverage, then explore Python and Docker.
In the Explorer, show all source datasets in the Source selector. Eurostat occupational
groups open under Roles; Skills correctly has ITBoard evidence only. Each source
keeps its own definition rather than pretending to measure the same population.
Switch to Count, then Data. Show original dates, counts, denominators and sources.
Explain: these are active-ad snapshots from one platform, not the whole Swiss market.

0:40–1:30 | Ask a question
AI Analyst: "How has Python changed in the available data?"
Follow up: "Compare with Docker."
Watch capability discovery and deterministic calculations update the working view.
In the last two snapshots, Python rises from 115/582 to 188/770.
Docker rises from 99 to 123 ads, but its share falls from 17.01% to 15.97%.
The denominator makes the interpretation more useful than a simple count chart.

1:30–2:05 | Test the limits
Ask: "And for junior developers?"
The analyst must explain that junior-by-skill data is unavailable and label any
broader junior indicator as a separate alternative. Missing is not zero.

2:05–2:40 | Support a decision
Ask: "Should Powercoders expand its AI training programme?"
The analyst can inspect the ML / AI tag and approved research notes, but must
also identify missing partner requirements, programme outcomes, costs and capacity.
Expect a cautious pilot or further evidence gathering, not a fabricated yes/no.

2:40–3:00 | Verify and continue
Open a cited result and its publisher link. Return to Explore this view.
Show that manual filters and AI use the same result, and that conversation
history and original results survive reload. Twenty follow-ups are supported.

The dataset is a frozen verified release, not a live feed. AI needs the server
API key; the manual Explorer remains available when AI is unavailable.
